Open Spaces' Principles
Whoever comes are the right people.
Whatever happens happens.
Whenever it starts is the right time. (within our schedule ;) )
When it’s over, it’s over.

The law of mobility

> If at any time you find yourself in any situation where you are neither learning nor contributing: go someplace else.
Bring your best self.
You have two ears, one mouth.
The person who offered the conversation should start but doesn’t always have to.
You don't have to be the expert, you can just be interested in the topic.
The open space doesn't have to be a tech talk.
Personalities
Bumblebees > They internalize the law of mobility quickly, and constantly flit from meeting to meeting, pollinating, cross-fertilizing, and adding richness and variety.
Butterflies > The may never get into any meeting, aka "The open spaces isn't their thing," they look at Open Spaces as an opportunity for an extended "hallway track."
Don't forget the closing circle.
